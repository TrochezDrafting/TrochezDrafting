Overview

Fast turnaround for a tight permit deadline.

A small commercial space needed existing-conditions documentation before a build-out could move through permitting. A same-week site visit captured accurate measurements of the shell space, existing MEP rough-in locations, and storefront conditions, translated into a clean drawing set the general contractor could hand directly to the permitting office.

  • 01Field measurement of the existing tenant shell space and storefront.
  • 02Documentation of existing MEP rough-in and structural column locations.
  • 03Drafted existing-conditions floor plan and reflected ceiling plan.
  • 04Delivered a coordinated drawing set within the contractor's permit timeline.
