OUR PROCESS FOR AS-BUILTS

From site visit to delivered drawings. Fulfill your needs within our process.

01 / Field Visit

Site measure

On-site measurement and documentation of existing conditions through photos or videos.

02 / Documentation

Field notes & photos

Detailed notes and photo reference organized for accurate translation into CAD.

03 / Drafting

CAD production

As-built drawings (plans, elevations, sections, schedules, etc.) drafted to your firm's layer standards and formats.

04 / Delivery

Review & handoff

Markup review in Bluebeam, revisions, and final delivery in your preferred file format like AutoCAD .dwg
